# XGBoost Model Training Summary

This report summarizes the feature engineering and training process for the XGBoost models.

## Feature Engineering (`tradestorun/feature_engineer.py`)

The feature engineering process involves calculating a variety of technical indicators. The base features are `open`, `high`, `low`, `close`, `volume`, `histogram`, `signal`, and `macd`.

The following indicators are calculated and added to the feature set:
- Simple Moving Averages (SMA) for 5 and 10 periods on the 'close' price.
- Candle Body Size.
- Relative Strength Index (RSI) with a window of 14.
- Lagged 'close' price features for 1 to 5 periods.
- Bollinger Bands with a window of 20 and 2 standard deviations.
- Stochastic Oscillator with a window of 14.
- Average True Range (ATR) with a window of 14.
- MACD with fast, slow, and signal periods of 12, 26, and 9 respectively.
- Ichimoku Cloud.
- ADX with a window of 14.

Rows with NaN values after feature engineering are dropped.

## Model Training (`tradestorun/2_train_model.py`)

The script trains an `xgboost.XGBClassifier` model.

- **Enabled Indicators by Default:**
  - "rsi": True
  - "stoch": True
  - "adx": True
  - "bbands": True
- **Data Splitting:** The data is split into training and validation sets with a 80/20 ratio, without shuffling.
- **Feature Scaling:** `StandardScaler` is used to scale the features.
- **XGBoost Parameters:**
  - objective: 'binary:logistic'
  - eval_metric: 'logloss'
  - use_label_encoder: False
  - n_estimators: 1000
  - learning_rate: 0.05
  - max_depth: 5
  - subsample: 0.8
  - colsample_bytree: 0.8
  - gamma: 0.1
- **Early Stopping:** The training uses early stopping with 50 rounds to prevent overfitting.
- **MLflow Tracking:** The script is instrumented with MLflow to log parameters, metrics, and artifacts, including the model, scaler, classification report, confusion matrix, and feature importance.
